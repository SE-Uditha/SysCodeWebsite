# Website imagery

## Home background

- `public/images/developer-workspace-background.png` is the user-supplied image, 1672 × 941, imported unchanged from `Cinematic Developer Workspace at Sunset.png`.
- The home section applies a CSS overlay mixing 50% brand orange and 50% white at 15% opacity. The image itself retains its original colors.

## Studio scenes

- `public/images/studio-collaboration-natural.webp` is the hero photograph, 1400 × 933.
- `public/images/design-workspace-natural.webp` is the homepage and About photograph, 1200 × 900.

These illustrative scenes were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, then edited to restore natural daylight, skin tones, and material colors. They do not depict SysCode employees. The earlier orange-tinted project copies were removed because the website uses the natural-color versions.
